Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the 50 ETH Soft Cap is not reached?

The presale smart contract includes built-in soft cap protection. If the 50 ETH soft cap is not met by the presale end date, participants can reclaim their contributed ETH directly from the verified presale contract.

How does the 21M Hard Burn Floor work?

OBSIDION launches with 120,000,000 $OBS. On-chain burns permanently reduce supply until it reaches 21,000,000 $OBS—at which point the smart contract automatically stops burning forever.

How do I view $OBS in my MetaMask wallet?

Make sure you are connected to Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 4663) and click the "+ Add $OBS" button in the top navigation bar to automatically import the verified token contract.

Where can I verify all smart contracts?

Every contract (Token, Presale, and Timelock) is publicly deployed on Robinhood Chain and linked directly to Blockscout in the presale panel above.
